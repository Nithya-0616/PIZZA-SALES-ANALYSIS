# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing pizza sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product preferences and revenue trends.

An interactive dashboard was developed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse overall revenue and order performance.
- Identify the best-performing pizzas.
- Understand sales trends across months.
- Identify peak ordering hours.
- Analyse pizza size and category contributions.
- Identify the most frequently used ingredients.
- Understand product preferences and purchasing patterns.

## Dataset Overview

The project uses a pizza sales dataset containing **48,620 records and 12 columns**, covering the period January to December 2015.

Key fields include:
- Order ID
- Pizza ID
- Pizza Name
- Order Date and Time
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Total Price
- Pizza Size
- Pizza Category
- Pizza Ingredients

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Top 5 Performing Pizzas
Identifies the pizzas generating the highest revenue.

### 2. Monthly Sales Trend
Tracks monthly revenue fluctuations and highlights changes in sales performance.

### 3. Peak Ordering Hours
Analyses order distribution throughout the day to identify high-demand periods.

### 4. Pizza Size Contribution
Compares the contribution of different pizza sizes to overall sales.

### 5. Top 5 Most Used Ingredients
Identifies the five most frequently used individual ingredients through a separate ingredient-level analysis.

### 6. Sales by Pizza Category
Compares revenue generated by different pizza categories, including Classic, Supreme, Chicken and Veggie.

## Data Transformation and Analysis

Power Query was used to prepare the dataset and create a separate ingredient-level analysis table.

The ingredient column originally contained multiple ingredients in a single field. It was split into individual rows, cleaned and grouped by ingredient, with quantities aggregated to identify the most frequently used ingredients.

The original sales table was retained separately to preserve transaction-level calculations.

DAX measures were created to calculate revenue, distinct orders, total pizzas sold and average pizzas per order.

## Key Business Insights

- The dataset records 21,350 unique orders and 49,574 pizzas sold.
- Total revenue amounts to approximately $817.86K.
- Average order value is $38.31.
- Customers purchase approximately 2.32 pizzas per order on average.
- The dashboard enables comparison of product performance, monthly revenue patterns and ordering-hour trends.
- Ingredient-level analysis provides a clearer understanding of commonly used pizza ingredients.
